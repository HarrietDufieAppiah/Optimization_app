About ECO-FAST

ECO-FAST is a research tool from the Department of Chemical Engineering at Rowan University. It uses an optimization model to help users make data-driven decisions for food waste treatment.

Who Uses It?

  • Researchers
  • Engineers
  • Municipal planners
  • Waste management companies
